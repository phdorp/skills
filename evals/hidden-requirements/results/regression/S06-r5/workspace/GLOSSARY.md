# birdlog

birdlog is a command-line tool for logging bird sightings. This glossary covers its single context.

## Language

**Birder**:
A person who logs sightings with birdlog.

**Sighting**:
One observation of a species at a place and time, with a count.

**Place**:
Where a sighting was made. A name is required; coordinates are optional.
_Avoid_: Location
